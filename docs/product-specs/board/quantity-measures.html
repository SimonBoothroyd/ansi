It is stored as a measure (that is what makes the nutrition line reverse) but it is stated in the ingredient’s Nutrition section, beside the figures it is printed per — a serving is the size a panel is printed per, not a size anybody cooks, plans or shops in, so no door offers it. Chip order: the row’s own measures — the one that weighs a piece first, then in the order the household dragged them → the default unit and the rest of its family → the other family, once a density unlocks it → the imprecise words, behind the divider. Measures lead because clove and can are words for this row and exist nowhere else, where g is offered on everything. The sheet opens on the first chip — the whole measure where one weighs a piece (ADR-0016), else the row’s first word, else the default unit, which is simply where the row’s own words run out. A surface reopening on something stored — a line being edited, a pack in the words it was last bought in — opens on that instead. A measure chip carries the bare word the household gave it; what one comes to is said once, on the line beside the amount (clove (3 g)). piece is the one chip that carries a figure, because the word alone would mean a clove or a bulb.
