What is the same. The row is the shipped three-part line — [amount] [identity] [note]; the amount cell opens the quantity sheet with its Optional switch, the identity cell opens the target picker, and ＋ Add ingredient adds. Nothing new is built

What is gone, and why it is gone rather than locked. The whole header form — title, serves, makes, times, shelf life, file under — and the whole method are not drawn. A control drawn and refused is a control you have to explain on every tap; a control absent is a mode you understand in one look. The method has a second reason: a step chip points at a line id, an added line has not got one, and an excluded line would orphan its chips

Serves survives as a sentence. Every amount on this list is per serves 4 — drop the number and the list stops being readable. So it is stated in the from the recipe strip with the step count and the title, in mono muted, with nothing in it to tap

No grip. A reorder is not storable this week, and a drag that silently reverts on reopen is worse than no drag. Group headings are inert text: no rename, no bin, no ＋ Add group. An added line lands at the foot of the list

An optional line wears no tag here. The row is name, note and — once it is changed — the week tag; Parmesan is optional in the recipe and says so only inside its amount sheet, whose Optional switch is the one place this screen states or changes it. The moment it is ticked in, the line carries this week · included like any other change

The tag is one line, and it is the old line. “this week · was 400 g Pork sausage” — what the recipe says, in the recipe's own words and the app's own name for the ingredient, so the reader can undo it in their head before they undo it with the button. It uses the optional tag's voice: one badge vocabulary on a recipe line, not two

An excluded line stays on the list, struck and muted, with its amount struck too. A removed row would be a hole: next Tuesday's reader could not see what is missing, and could not put it back. The bin excludes in week mode — the line is the recipe's, and this screen cannot delete the recipe's line — and an already-excluded row draws no bin at all: its reset is the only way back. Ticking a counted line optional in the amount sheet is the same exclusion from the other side

An added line has no recipe line behind it, so its control is ✕ remove rather than ↺ reset — there is nothing to go back to. Same slot, same voice, honest verb

The reset is per line and takes no confirm — the dish row's − idiom: muted, not red. It is a draft action; Save is still what stores it, so the undo is ‹

“Back to the recipe” states its own count and sits at the foot, after every line, in the destructive voice. It clears the draft's overrides; Save commits the clearing. A reset that cannot say how much it undoes gets pressed blind — this one can, because the variant is one object

Nothing is said on the recipe page. The page is reached from the Library as often as from the Week, and the Library holds no viewed week — a badge there would have to answer which week? with a guess. Every surface that has a week says it: the Week grid, Shop and Cook
