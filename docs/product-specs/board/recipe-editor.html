one row layout everywhere: [amount] [name] [note] on a single line, the amount in its own 84 px column so every identity left-aligns. It is what the recipe page — the screen a cook reads — already prints. The amount cell opens the quantity sheet and the name cell opens the identity picker: the same two doors, side by side instead of stacked

used in N steps is a second muted line under the name, and only when N > 0 — it is a fact about the line, not a control

the grip ⋮⋮ is the only thing that drags. Long-press-anywhere would turn a scroll into an accidental move on a list whose whole job is tapping

a line dropped under a heading is filed under that heading. It keeps its id, so every method chip pointing at it still does — which is the whole reason the gesture is worth having, since delete + re-add already reordered a list and cost every reference. Headings do not drag; a group left empty by a move is kept

the doors sit tight under the last line, and a line added lands in the last group — the drag then puts it where it belongs
