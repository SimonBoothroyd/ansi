the macro line appends · 2 on a row that states one, and prints nothing at all on a row that does not — fibre is the one optional figure, and its absence is an absence, never a zero

Where the line is dense the two units that never change are also the two longest, so energy is a flame and fibre a sheaf after their figures. Each glyph carries the word it replaced to a screen reader, and the words stay wherever there is room for them — the recipe panel’s cells, and the form’s own field labels.

the picker’s rows carry no source line. The manager and the import review both name the food behind a filled row (the USDA description, or the pack a barcode was scanned off); this one deliberately does not — it is a search surface, and a description under every row is noise while you are typing

the footer carries two doors, because the thing a line wants may be a recipe nobody has written yet. Both push over this sheet — the ingredient form, or the recipe editor seeded with the typed words — and the sheet stays open underneath to resolve with what they popped. Back out of either and nothing was written.

No placeholder, no status column: an empty recipe is a real recipe that simply yields nothing yet — it contributes nothing to macros or the shop list, and a component whose target states no yield reads in batches. The new editor’s own picker offers the same two doors, as deep as the cook goes.
