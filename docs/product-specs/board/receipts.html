A settled line is one sentence with the money first, and the note under the name is how many, the pack, and what they come to per basis. A line sold by weight needs no pack — the paper printed the pound and the rate. A discount printed under an item is folded into that item’s sum and shown as a deduction on the same line. Open, every card leads with the money and the name it resolved to, keeps the verbatim line under it, and carries a PRICE chip on the figure that was read; a line with no figure gets the louder door instead. Not food sits at the foot of every open card and moves the line under the fold, where it counts toward the total and never toward a price, with it is food as the way back.

The vocabulary learns nothing, and the household remembers everything. Confirming a match writes no alias, so TJ ORG BANANAS never becomes a word the recipe door can see. What the next receipt inherits is this household’s own answers, filed under the printed name: the pack these words were last bought in for this row — falling back to the pack the row was last bought in anywhere — and the match, recalled per printed name off their own saved receipt lines, exact and never fuzzy, the most recent answer winning. A line that arrived that way says as you matched it last time beside its chosen row, and changing it is the correction.
