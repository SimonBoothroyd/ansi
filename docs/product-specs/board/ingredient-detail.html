An empty fibre field costs the row nothing. The dock is live and the row completes on the four — most labels outside the US print no fibre at all, and a row that waited on one would never be finished. Typing one stores it beside the four; leaving it blank stores nothing, never a zero (invariant 3), and a stated 0 is a reading that means something different.

Fibre alone is refused. A number in this field with the four blank says Fibre is an extra figure on a panel, not a panel of its own — give it a number beside the four, or clear it. It qualifies a panel; it is not one.

Where the figure goes on from here: the row’s macro line gains · 3 fibre, a recipe’s panel gains a fifth cell, and the week’s band gains a fib cell — each of them only when every figure behind it stated fibre. A total short by an unknown amount is the number invariant 3 forbids, so the recipe panel says fibre not counted · 2 lines without it: Onion, Stock instead.
