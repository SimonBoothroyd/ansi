Nothing on this screen is new. The switcher, the lens, the day card, the cook marker and the add line are the ones above — the week simply begins on Sunday and ends on Saturday, and its title dates it from the Sunday. What that buys is the whole point: the shop you do on Sunday morning is the shop for the week whose first dinner is that night, so a Sunday meal is no longer stranded at the tail of the week that is ending. The cook marker follows it without being taught anything — the cook day is the earliest day the batch covers, so a batch cooked on shopping day now heads the week instead of trailing Monday’s.

The order is a household fact, set once in Account (D5), not a per-phone view: it decides which week a meal is stored in, so both phones must agree or they would write into two different weeks. Monday-start is unchanged and stays the default — this frame is the same screen under the other setting.

the matrix that stood here is gone. It fitted seven days into one screenful and paid for it in an 11 px clipped line per meal, a border round every one of them and from Monday’s batch repeated on every card that shared a pot. A desk has vertical room and a scroll wheel, so the wide week does not have to be a calendar: clarity over compactness, the owner’s words. The left pane is a fixed 560 and draws ONE day at reading size — today by default, or whichever day the agenda’s › last pointed at. That choice is view state: nothing persists it, so coming back to the Week lands on today again, under Everyone.

the pane says everything the phone row says, at reading size, and one thing the phone has no room for: each meal’s own macros, as served to the people eating it — the recipe’s per-serving figure times the portions planned, which is why the ragù’s three portions read higher than the two single-eater lunches, and why the three lines add up to the ledger exactly. Both are read through one function, so a part and the sum cannot disagree. Figures are spelt out here — protein 144 g, not 144P — because the phone’s strip is compressed for a 320 px line and this pane is 560. The ledger is pinned to the foot: the meals scroll past it and the numbers stay put.

the agenda is the whole week, and it scrolls. Each day is a heading, one mono line carrying its energy and its mandatory denominator, then its meals as single Spectral lines under small-caps slot labels — wrapping allowed, truncation never. An eater initial prints only where the meal is not for everyone: J on the salmon, A on the frittata, nothing on the meals both eat. Every line keeps its −, every day keeps its one ＋ add a meal in every state, and the one open at left keeps the herb rule and no › — there is nowhere for it to go.

the batch story leaves the right pane, deliberately. No cook marker, no batch tick, no leader column, no per-day grams. Nothing on the agenda says Friday’s ragù is Tuesday’s pot; that reads in the pane, one tap away, and in Cook. Clarity was bought by moving the relationship rather than by stating it better, and that is the trade taken. The refusals still read on both sides in the app’s existing words: Saturday states no total — Sausage Sliders · 1 stub line and draws no figure at all, Sunday counts 1 of 2 meals, and an empty Wednesday says no meals — never a zero. Nothing here is draggable: week v3 has no move.
