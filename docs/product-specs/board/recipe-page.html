Two tabs, a scaler, and one per-serving panel closing the ingredients tab — with a total, or with the reason there isn’t one. The ⋯ menu can also print each line’s own macros under its name. The page is set as reading matter: serif title, sans lines, mono figures, kitchen fractions as glyphs (1½), and one filled shape — the chip row — above the tabs. Opened from a week it draws that week’s lines, and the optional tag is the switch that ticks a line in for it; from the Library the tag is a tag.

Imprecise and optional lines are left out by rule, not by failure.

the tag wears the sub-recipe chip’s shape — a 6 px box in the herb wash, small mono — after the note, never in the amount column. Two lines of the same grammar should not wear two geometries

the tag is also the whole statement: with line macros on, a tagged row prints nothing in its macro slot, because optional twice on one line is once too many

A line whose ingredient has been retired wears that same tag slot, reading ingredient removed · pick again, with the row's last known name muted beside it — the page's one broken-link voice, shared with linked recipe missing. Here the name stops being a door (a retired row has no page), the panel names the line the way it names a stub, and the fix is the editor's identity picker one posture over. Dropping the line would be the real failure: a recipe silently one line short

optional has two owners. The recipe says may be skipped — the author’s claim. The week says this time, yes — the cook’s. There is no third “usually yes”: a household that always wants the lime unticks Optional in the editor, because for them it is not optional

the tag is the switch, and its shape says so: an empty ring before the word, the way a box somebody may tick is drawn; ticked, the box fills herb with a check and reads included. Same radius, same padding — the answer changes without the line moving. The badge is 10 pt, so the tap target is padded out to 44 px rather than the badge drawn bigger

the rest is week mode’s grammar, read-only: a replaced line at the week’s own amount (500 g, still scaling with the servings control), a line it leaves out struck and muted — amount and name alike, and neither is a door — and an added line after the last group, because an override carries no group and no derivation reads one

the panel is the week’s own re-summation, not the Library’s figure, so ticking a line in recounts it as you watch. A third row names what came in — INCLUDED · names · for this week, its label in herb because it is the one row naming lines the total does cover — and it stands outside the caption, which is about what stayed out

the band gains · edited for this week on the first include, through the placement watch that was already there. No new route and no new screen

From the Library the tag is not a switch. A reading posture that writes nothing was drawn and rejected in the same conversation: a control that persists from one door and evaporates from another is a smell, and where there is no week there is no decision to make
