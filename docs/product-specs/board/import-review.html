The stages are real and they run in this order; the clock is the estimate. A single non-streaming call cannot report where the server got to, so each rung is timed off the extraction runs and written to stay true if it lands early or late — no rung claims a stage has finished.

the photo door has two model calls — the pages are transcribed, then the recipe is written out — which is why its ladder is twice as long and why it is the door that outlives a short client deadline

The wait ends one of three ways, and all three come back to the intake form with the reason under it: the review screen, “that site blocked the fetch … — try the photo import instead”, or “the model took too long … nothing has been saved, so it is safe to try again”. Nothing is written until Save at review, so every failure here is safe to repeat.

＋ ingredient ＋ section

the doors sit tight under the last line — they belong to the list, not to the screen. A gap there reads as a section break that is not there. A line added lands in the last section, so ＋ section then ＋ ingredient is one gesture

deleting a heading never deletes its lines — they move up into the section above, and down into what becomes the first when the first goes. Dropping food is what the line’s own 🗑 already does, so there is no confirm: nothing is lost. The last section standing loses its heading rather than going

a heading appears as a field wherever there is one to name — a single unnamed section shows no row at all, because that is the ordinary shape of a recipe that never divided its ingredients, and ＋ section is the way out of it

a line added here says added here — not on the page where the others print from source:. The honesty rule cuts both ways. Its flat index is minted past the payload’s last, so nothing renumbers and every chip already written still points where it did

the grip ⋮⋮ drags a line, and nothing else does. Dropping it under a heading files it there, so reordering and moving between sections are one gesture — the recipe editor's list, over the review's own state

a moved line keeps its flat index and changes only its position. The index is what resolutions are keyed by and what every step chip points at; the position is what commits as sort_order. Order and identity have stopped being the same number

the grip is on collapsed rows only — an open card is a form, not a row — and an open card closes the moment a drag starts elsewhere, so what crosses the list is always a row like every other row

re-matching a line relabels every chip pointing at it — the editor’s own notice and its one-tap revert, switched on here with no new UI. The ref never moves; only the printed word does, because a chip must never name something the recipe does not contain

renaming a chip in the chip sheet’s Word field keeps the chip. Typing over a chip’s own characters in the sentence still ends it — editing a span’s letters really is ending it, and that one is by design
