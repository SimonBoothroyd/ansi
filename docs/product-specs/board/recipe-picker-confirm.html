The condition is all four clauses: something is typed, no recipe title hit at any tier (typos included), no food hit — even a guessed one is an answer — and the vocabulary search has caught up with what is typed, so the row cannot flash between keystrokes offering to plan half a word. The list above says its own empty state; the third answer sits in the footer, above the door it is a sibling of. Tapping it resolves the picker to the words, trimmed: nothing is minted in the Library or the vocabulary, and the confirm sheet is next.

A bare shelf and a search that missed are two different sentences. No recipes yet is what a household with no Library reads. The door is under it. Once words are typed the list says them back — and names the shelf it emptied, because Favorites missing a dish is not the Library missing it: Nothing in your favorites matches “…”. Neither line offers anything; the miss has exactly one offer, and it is the footer's.
