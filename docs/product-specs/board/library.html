A book is a heading row: the fold’s own chevron, the name in Spectral, a dotted leader into the counts column that carries the phone’s own line — 11 recipes · 3 sections, or no recipes yet — and the book’s ⋯ as an inline glyph, the same menu the phone’s herb band hangs and the book page’s header bar wears. The name is a name, not a door: there is nothing behind it left to see. The fold is the phone’s, per book, per device, and it is the one thing that hides anything: Baking is shut here and shows its heading alone, which is why the count line has to read the same open or shut. Open is the default.

An open book lists every section it keeps and every recipe under each, Unsectioned last. A section is one heading line — the phone’s italic label, a leader into the same counts column, what it holds (4 recipes, or no recipes yet), then the ＋ that files a recipe into it and the section’s ⋯; Unsectioned carries the ＋ and no ⋯, exactly as on the card. A recipe is one line, indented from its section, with its second line set into the counts column rather than dropped — Kimchi Fried Rice has a stub in it, so it still says serves 2 and nothing else. Three levels, read as three from their indents: no box, no band, no rule but the hairline that closes a book.

Nothing counts the rest, because there is no rest — no remainder row, and no door out to /books/:id. A laptop has the room: “one Library screen showing everything is the better flavour.” It is long and it scrolls, which is what a desk does, and it is one lazy list of lines, so twenty-five books cost only the lines in the window.

The A–Z margin is what makes that hold: a letter with a book on the shelf is lit and scrolls the ledger to it, the rest are drawn in the hairline’s ink. It indexes first letters, not order — the books keep the household’s own, which is what Move up and Move down write. # takes a book that starts with a digit.

differs: the field is the app’s one search field at 300 px — Forui’s bordered box, not the underline drawn here. An underline would be a second field anatomy for one screen, and the field is shared with the ingredients manager and every picker
