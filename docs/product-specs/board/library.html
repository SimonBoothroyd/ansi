A tile is 180 px, always — a 70 px band over the 108 px the body gets. The band carries the book’s name and the phone’s own count line, 42 recipes · 3 sections, or no recipes yet where the shelf is empty; a name past the band’s two lines is clipped there rather than allowed to shorten the body.

differs: the grid asks for as many columns as fit at a 320 px tile — four across the 1160 pane a 1440 window leaves beside the sidebar, three on an iPad in landscape. Both of those are the shell's pane: until it hands a branch root the whole width, the tab shell centres the Library at the 640 measure and the same grid draws two columns. The header's 👥 is likewise the shell's to remove, and it is still there

What the body lists is titles. The Library’s recipe row is two lines — the title, then serves 4 · 520 kcal · 28 g protein — and it carries its own ⋯; three of those do not fit 108 px, and a row that drops its second line is a row the app does not have. So the shelf lists what is on it, and the book page a tile opens draws the row, as the search column below does. The ★ stays: it reports, it never files, and a title past the tile’s width is clipped here — where it is a row, it wraps instead.
