The same three columns, because it is the same job. The recipe review’s width was spent so the page could simply be there beside what was made of it; a receipt asks the identical question of every line, so the photos stand where the page stood and the selected line lights its printed words. The receipt’s own facts — store, date, printed totals, the join card — head the lines column as the header form heads the recipe’s, and scroll with it. The pack door lives in the panel, where the expanded card’s controls live. The commit bar is the lines column’s footer and reads the one map.

Reviewed anywhere, scanned on the phone. The photo import is gated on web, so the desk never opens the camera: a receipt shot on the phone is reviewed on whichever screen is nearest, and a saved receipt opens from the ledger on this same editable review.
