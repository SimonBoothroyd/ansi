These frames were redrawn line-by-line from what step 8 actually shipped (import_view.dart, reconciliation_view.dart, recon_line_card.dart, method_step_text.dart). What survives from the original design: the three-part line (amount · ingredient · notes), resolve-once identity, the seeded picker that never opens blank, the 7.7 quantity + unit-chip sheet as the one amount editor, and a read-only method in v1.
